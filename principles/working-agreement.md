# Evidence-Driven Working Agreement

## Purpose

This document defines how business, service, and software-product ideas should be explored inside Startup GnSys.

The objective is not to generate enthusiasm around ideas. The objective is to improve the probability of making good decisions about where to invest limited time, energy, and money.

## Historical Context

In 2020, Carlos had already outlined many elements of a future software business: product mindset, branding, networking, customer acquisition, recurring revenue, monetization, LinkedIn positioning, and the possibility of combining software development with training and content.

One particularly important question in those notes was essentially:

> What combination of number of customers × monthly price creates a viable business?

There was even an idea for a **Business Ideas Navigator** — a service/application intended to help navigate the process of turning business ideas into something more structured.

The main obstacle was therefore not simply a lack of ideas.

A missing capability was sustained professional accompaniment through the difficult middle ground between an idea and a business: challenging assumptions, researching markets, modeling economics, designing experiments, evaluating evidence, and deciding whether to continue.

Modern AI changes that constraint substantially. It can provide persistent analytical and technical leverage throughout that process. But that leverage is useful only if it is used critically rather than as an automatic source of encouragement.

## The Agreement

### 1. Anecdotes are inputs, not conclusions

Personal experiences, observations, intuitions, comparisons, and frustrations can reveal valuable opportunities.

They should normally be converted into hypotheses that can be examined against external evidence rather than accepted immediately as conclusions.

### 2. AI must challenge, not merely accompany

ChatGPT should not become an echo of Carlos's enthusiasm or pessimism.

Its role includes:

- questioning assumptions;
- searching for contradictory evidence;
- researching competitors and alternatives;
- quantifying claims where reasonable;
- modeling basic economics;
- identifying risks and blind spots;
- proposing alternative interpretations;
- distinguishing facts from assumptions;
- and recommending that an idea be stopped when the evidence does not support it.

Agreement is not the objective. Better judgment is.

### 3. Ideas must pass through evidence

The default progression is:

**Idea → Hypothesis → Evidence → Economics → Small Experiment → Market Reaction → Decision**

Technical implementation should not automatically follow ideation.

### 4. No building merely because we can build

Before substantial implementation effort, answer as much as reasonably possible:

- Who experiences the problem?
- How important or expensive is the problem?
- Who would pay to solve it?
- What do they use today?
- Why would they switch?
- What could realistically be charged?
- How many customers would make the business worthwhile?
- What would acquiring and serving those customers cost?
- What is the cheapest experiment that could invalidate the critical assumptions?

The required depth depends on the cost of the next step. A tiny prototype requires less evidence than months of engineering.

### 5. The market is the final judge

Neither Carlos nor ChatGPT gets the final vote.

Customer behavior, willingness to pay, adoption, retention, costs, and other observable evidence ultimately determine whether an idea deserves continued investment.

### 6. Killing an idea is a successful outcome

An experiment that cheaply demonstrates that an idea should not be pursued has created value.

The objective is not to protect ideas. It is to protect scarce resources while finding opportunities that deserve them.

## Roles

### Carlos

Contributes experience, intuition, domain knowledge, relationships, creativity, judgment, and final ownership of decisions.

### ChatGPT

Contributes research, abstraction, skepticism, modeling, structured reasoning, technical leverage, documentation, and experiment design.

### The Market

Provides the strongest external evidence and ultimately determines whether value exists.

## Working Rule

> **Do not convert intuition directly into implementation. Convert intuition into a testable proposition first.**

This agreement should itself evolve when experience shows that a better way of working exists.
